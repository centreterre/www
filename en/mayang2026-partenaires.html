Become a partner of Mayang 2026

We have the rare -and one might even say unique- privilege of setting foot on the last virgin lands on Earth, much as in the bygone era of the great geographic expeditions, but now bolstered by 21st-century technology.

Our ambition is to push the boundaries of knowledge by exploring ever farther, and to share our findings with those who cannot venture into these extreme regions-scientists, the general public, and especially young people, in whom it is vital to instill an entrepreneurial spirit.

If these values are yours, if exploring, understanding, and sharing resonate within you, join us in this great human adventure.

La Société des Explorateurs Français

The Société des Explorateurs Français (SEF) was founded in 1937 by renowned explorers. The SEF brings together individuals with truly extraordinary backgrounds. It is open to anyone who shares the belief that life itself is an adventure. Its members dedicate their lives to exploration, discovery in all its forms, and the sharing of knowledge.
The Société des Explorateurs Français (SEF) contributes to knowledge of little-known regions of the Earth. It welcomes men and women who often propose original research and approaches in areas that are very difficult to access, who approach forgotten tribes or conquer untouched peaks, who attempt extreme sea routes or navigate little-traveled rivers, visit the most impenetrable jungles, discover the underground world and the hidden faces of our planet, or participate in the exploration of the solar system or the infinitely small.
All these men and women, representing so many disciplines, share a keen interest in the exchange between exploration and research, in adventure, in writing and in capturing images of the world.
