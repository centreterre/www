Fédération française d'études et de sports sous-marins
https://www.ffessm.fr/
The French Federation for underwater study and sport is a state-approved organization and a delegate of the French Ministry of Sports for underwater activities. Founded in 1948, the FFESSM is a multi-disciplinary organization offering the ideal framework for scuba diving, free diving and underwater sports, with diplomas and certificates recognized worldwide through the World Confederation of Underwater Activities (CMAS).
