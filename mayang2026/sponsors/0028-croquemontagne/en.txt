Croque Montagne
https://www.croque-montagne.fr/
After 10 years in a sports store in a Vercors resort, Gaëtan, a child of the plateau, took over Croque Montagne, a climbing store in Pont en Royans at the foot of the Presles cliffs, in 2004. In 2013, after 2 moves, Croque Montagne moved into a brand-new, welcoming building. This bright building houses the entire Croque Montagne business: the store, rental and online sales. The store specializes in :
<br>- Technical equipment for caving, canyoning, climbing, mountaineering, skiing and ski touring.
<br>- Clothing, footwear, tents, accessories and winter and summer gear: hiking, camping, bivouacs, skiing, etc.
<br>- Travel essentials: rucksacks, suitcases, accessories and protection.
<br>- Documentation: topos, maps and other books on mountaineering, rescue, caving, canyoning and working at height.
