Moroccan Explorers is a young, multidisciplinary, and independent team made up of enthusiasts eager for adventure, discovery, and above all, wonder. a team that strives, however modest its means may be, to reveal through its activities another facet of Morocco, a country that, like so many others, boasts natural riches as magnificent as they are fragile—riches that must be better known in order to be better preserved.
