Comité de Spéléologie, Rhône et Métropole de Lyon
http://www.csr-rhonealpes.fr/cds69
Rhône Departemental Comitee for Caving (the metropolitan area of Lyon included) with 288 members in 2019 (4.2% of national federated members), CDS 69 is the second largest in France in terms of federated members. Its members benefit from the proximity of the Lyon metropolitan area to the karst wealth of the Rhône-Alpes region, whose massifs are among the richest in France in terms of natural caves and canyons.
