Asociación Espeleológica de Patagonia
https://espeleopatagonia.cl
The Patagonian Speleological Association is a Chilean non-profit organization. Its purpose is to develop speleology in Chile and bring together speleologists and scientific researchers with the aim of preparing and conducting speleological, geographical, and scientific expeditions for the exploration of karst, pseudokarst, and cryokarst environments, among others.
