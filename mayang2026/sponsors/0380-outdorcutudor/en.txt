Outdoor cu Tudor

The story of Outdoor cu Tudor began in the summer of 1988, with the first expeditions into the caves and mountains of Romania.
Since then, this passion has grown organically, with each expedition to unspoiled places such as the caves of Laos, Vietnam, and Papua New Guinea, each exploration of caves in Romania and other countries, and each cave diving, canyoning, or mountaineering trip.
We love mountains, water, adventure, exploring nature, and our inner selves. We are passionate and qualified instructors. We are convinced that, through experiences in the great outdoors, we can get to know ourselves better and become closer to ourselves.
Since 2016, this passion has taken shape in the form of a company offering structured programs.
