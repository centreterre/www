Fédération Spéléologique Européenne (FSE) – Eurospeleo Projects
https://www.eurospeleo.eu/
The European Speleological Federation (ESF), founded in 1990 under the patronage of the International Union of Speleology, brings together representatives of national speleological federations and associations from across Europe. The ESF, equipped with appropriate resources and structures (committees, working groups, events, etc.), encourages the practice of caving in all its forms: exploration expeditions, scientific conferences, educational or artistic projects, etc. The ESF's EuroSpeleo Projects support gatherings of European cavers from all walks of life in the development of new creative and dynamic caving projects.
