Aquadesign
https://aquadesign.eu
Aquadesign is a French brand specializing in technical nautical equipment for whitewater and adventure sports: hydrospeed, kayaking, rafting, and safety. For over 30 years, Aquadesign has been applying its expertise to technical equipment designed for exploration and demanding uses. Aquadesign is proud to be a partner of the Centre Terre association and to support the Mayang 2026 expedition by providing two hydrospeeds for the progression and exploration phases in aquatic environments.
