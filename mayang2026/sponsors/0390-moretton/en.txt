Specializing in difficult access and high-altitude work, LAURENT MORETTON Travaux Acrobatiques has been supporting professionals and local authorities in their most complex projects for over 20 years.
When traditional solutions (scaffolding, aerial work platforms) are not suitable, our teams work safely using ropes to provide appropriate technical solutions.
We operate in the construction, industrial, and environmental sectors throughout the Rhône-Alpes region and mainland France. Our technicians, who come from a variety of trades, bring their expertise to bear on high-rise structures and demanding sites.
Thanks to our highly qualified teams trained in working at height, we carry out operations where safety, quality of execution, and performance are combined with efficiency.
