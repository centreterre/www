La Société des Explorateurs Français
https://www.societe-explorateurs.org/
The Société des Explorateurs Français (SEF) was founded in 1937 by renowned explorers. The SEF brings together individuals with truly extraordinary backgrounds. It is open to anyone who shares the belief that life itself is an adventure. Its members dedicate their lives to exploration, discovery in all its forms, and the sharing of knowledge.
<br>The Société des Explorateurs Français (SEF) contributes to knowledge of little-known regions of the Earth. It welcomes men and women who often propose original research and approaches in areas that are very difficult to access, who approach forgotten tribes or conquer untouched peaks, who attempt extreme sea routes or navigate little-traveled rivers, visit the most impenetrable jungles, discover the underground world and the hidden faces of our planet, or participate in the exploration of the solar system or the infinitely small.
<br>All these men and women, representing so many disciplines, share a keen interest in the exchange between exploration and research, in adventure, in writing and in capturing images of the world.
